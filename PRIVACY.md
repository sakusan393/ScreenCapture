# Privacy policy

ScreenCapture does not collect analytics, diagnostics, crash reports, or other
personal information. It does not transfer information to networked systems.

The application performs the following local operations only:

- captures pixels from screens or windows selected by the user;
- reads images from and writes composed images to the Windows clipboard when
  requested by the user;
- saves images to a path selected by the user;
- stores hotkey and editing preferences under `%APPDATA%\ScreenCapture`;
- uses a local named pipe to ask an already-running ScreenCapture process to
  start a new capture.

ScreenCapture does not include telemetry or network services. Its third-party
components are used locally and do not transfer user information on behalf of
ScreenCapture.

Questions or reports about this policy can be submitted through the
[ScreenCapture issue tracker](https://github.com/sakusan393/ScreenCapture/issues).
